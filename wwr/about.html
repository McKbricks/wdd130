Racing Rapids

Our mission is to help families and friends explore red rock canyons, learn to paddle together, and enjoy a day on the river with experienced guides.

History

Racing Rapids began when two local river guides started taking small groups through the canyons they grew up exploring.

Their weekend trips grew into a season of guided adventures for first-time rafters and returning paddlers.
